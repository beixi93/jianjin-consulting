Booking & Payment

  • Send a booking request by email with your preferred dates and a short description of your topic.
  • You will receive a confirmation and a secure payment link.
  • Payment is made in advance by credit or debit card through Stripe. Card details are processed by Stripe and are never stored by us.
  • After payment, you receive the video call link for your session.

Cancellation & Refunds

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled session receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session, or missed sessions, are not refundable. One free rescheduling is offered instead.
  • If a session cannot take place for reasons on our side, you receive a full refund or a new appointment, at your choice.
  • For project-based work, refunds for work not yet delivered are handled as agreed in the written project offer.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
